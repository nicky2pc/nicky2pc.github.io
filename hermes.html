Hermes Personal Agent

A private, owner-operated assistant maintained by Nick Y. This application is for personal use and is not an official Google or Nous Research service.

Google integration

With the account owner's permission, the assistant reads Gmail messages for summaries and accesses Google Calendar to review schedules, create or update events on request, and deliver reminders. It is not a public sign-up service.

Selected information may be processed by the configured AI service provider and delivered to the owner's connected messaging account.
