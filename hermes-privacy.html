Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal Google integration of Hermes Personal Agent operated by Nick Y. Contact: explore3web@gmail.com.

Data accessed and purpose

Depending on permissions granted, the assistant accesses Gmail message metadata and content, and Google Calendar event details. These are used to answer the owner's requests, summarize messages, manage calendar events, and provide reminders. OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's server to maintain authorized access.

Processing and disclosure

Data is processed on the owner's hosted server. Relevant excerpts may be sent to configured AI API providers to generate responses, and summaries or reminders may be sent to the owner's connected messaging services, such as Telegram. Those services process transmitted information under their own terms and privacy policies. This public website does not host the owner's Google data or OAuth credentials.

Storage and retention

Conversation history, outputs, caches, and operational logs may retain information used by the assistant until the owner deletes them. Credentials are retained until removed or replaced. Revoking Google access prevents future authorized retrieval but does not automatically delete previously stored records.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access at Google Account connections and remove credentials and stored records from the assistant's server. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to the contact above.

Scope

This application is intended for the operator's personal use, not public enrollment. This policy covers this integration, not the policies of Google, the server host, AI providers, or messaging services.
